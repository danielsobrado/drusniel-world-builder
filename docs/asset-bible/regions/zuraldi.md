# Zuraldi — autonomous mountain valleys of Lumeshire

**Published lore:** https://www.drusniel.com/the-zuraldarr/
**Public realm:** Zuraldi highlands (within Lumeshire)  
**Map-state:** Lumeshire  
**Culture(s):** `zuraldarr`  
**Candidate branches:** pass-wardens / valley-households / caravan-zuraldarr  
**Landscape:** dense upland forest, gray craggy peaks, terraced valley plots, narrow caravan shelf roads  
**Economy and roles:** cattle, salted meat, terrace agriculture, mountain ponies, Stonehold-bound caravan exchange, ancestral councils  
**Geometry grammar:** heavy irregular gray stone retaining walls, dark timber frame, deep low slate roofs pitched for wind and rain, siting stepped into steep terrain, rough but engineered terraces  
**Canon vs proposal:** Published: Zuraldi semi-autonomous under Lumeshire, six matriarchal offices, cliff-side caravan trade, mountain farming, groves, witches and druids. Design proposal: individual building geometry.
