# Frostgard — Norths and winter frontier

**Published lore:** https://www.drusniel.com/ice-and-iron-the-warriors-of-the-empire-of-frostgard/
**Public realm:** Empire of Frostgard  
**Map-state:** Frostgard  
**Culture(s):** `norths`  
**Candidate branches:** frostgard-crown / sea-road-norths / giant-border-norths  
**Landscape:** snowy mountain foothills, windswept conifer forests, frozen rivers, sheltered fjord harbors  
**Economy and roles:** winter stores, timber, fishing, furs, defense of giant border, longhall governance  
**Geometry grammar:** horizontal heavy-hewn timber, deep steep slate-or-sod pitched roofs, tall smoke vents, rugged stone plinths, windbreak courtyards  
**Canon vs proposal:** Published: Jotunheim gates, great halls/skalds, snow supply caravans, frontier giant threat and missing villages. Exact coastal architecture is design proposal informed by game culture.
