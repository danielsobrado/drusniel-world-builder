# Lumeshire — Imperial heartland and marches

**Published lore:** https://www.drusniel.com/the-resilient-defense-of-the-lumeshirean-divine-empire/
**Public realm:** Lumeshirean Divine Empire  
**Map-state:** Lumeshire  
**Culture(s):** `lumish`  
**Candidate branches:** imperial-heartlanders / fortified-provincials / tideward-lumish  
**Landscape:** temperate farmlands, maintained highways, river crossings, defended forest marches  
**Economy and roles:** civic administration, grain, church institutions, road trade, military logistics
