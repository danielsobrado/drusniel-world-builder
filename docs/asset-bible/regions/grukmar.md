# Grukmar — multi-peoples, bog networks and strongholds

**Published lore:** https://www.drusniel.com/the-unruly-might-of-the-grukmar-tribes/
**Public realm:** Grukmar Tribes  
**Map-state:** Grukmar  
**Culture(s):** `orc-peoples / goblin-peoples / rakhnid-broods`  
**Candidate branches:** grukmar-orcs / grukmar-goblins / grukmar-rakhnid; local clan and town affiliations  
**Landscape:** southern frontier marshes and bogs, salvage streams, grassland uplands, wooded fortified ridges  
**Economy and roles:** barter, caravan logistics, salvage crafts, fishing/foraging, clan assemblies, intermittent warfare and fortified trading  
**Geometry grammar:** intentionally multiple grammars: marsh goblin settlements use elevated timber piles and reed roofs; upland orc towns use low broad sturdy timber-and-stone halls; mixed markets combine trade modules on raised dry ground  
**Canon vs proposal:** Published: territory with orcs, goblins and unknown deep-bog dangers, conflicting factions and raids; no universal shared ruler or uniform settlement design. Multiple local economic identities from repo-authored cultural seeds.
