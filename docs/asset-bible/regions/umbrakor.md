# Umbra'kor — sunless dominion and depth wards

**Published lore:** https://www.drusniel.com/the-umbrakor-dominions-pact-with-darkness/
**Public realm:** Umbra'kor Dominion  
**Map-state:** Umbra'kor  
**Culture(s):** `umbra-kori`  
**Candidate branches:** metasadhell-civic / deep-house-umbra / warden-umbra / surface-enclave-umbra  
**Landscape:** vast underground chambers, vertical basalt escarpments, subterranean water, fungus agriculture, heavily guarded rift approaches  
**Economy and roles:** house compounds, temple archives, fungus production, intelligence, toll trade and depth defense  
**Geometry grammar:** dark basalt arched stone and cliff-carved tiers, narrow vertical bridges over caverns, iron walkway structure, discreet pale phosphorescent lines and controlled light pockets
