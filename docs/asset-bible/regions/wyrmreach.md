**Published lore:** https://www.drusniel.com/the-mysteries-of-the-wyrmreach-dominion/

**Map-state:** Wyrmreach  
**Culture(s):** `multiple local seeds: orc-peoples, goblin-peoples, jotunn, serpent-peoples, rakhnid-broods; rare draconic-remnant`  
**Candidate branches:** wyrmreach-orcs / wyrmreach-goblins / wyrmreach-jotunn / locally unmapped communities
