# Stonehold — mountain forges and Vondari fortress cities

**Published lore:** https://www.drusniel.com/forge-and-fire-the-mountain-kingdom-of-stoneholds-stand-against-chaos/
**Public realm:** Mountain Kingdom of Stonehold  
**Map-state:** Ironpeak (working map-state crosswalk; unverified alias)  
**Culture(s):** `vondari`  
**Candidate branches:** crown-forge-vondari / mining-settlement branches  
**Landscape:** bare high mountain rock, deep mines, cavern avenues, steep pass roads, fortified rock faces  
**Economy and roles:** mining, foundries, metalwork, engineering, stone trade, council and temple oversight  
**Geometry grammar:** carved massive granite/basalt facades, stout squared arches, reinforced vaulted passages, iron/bronze gates, chimney stacks and integrated ore cranes
