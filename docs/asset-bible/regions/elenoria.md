# Elenoria — living forest cities

**Published lore:** https://www.drusniel.com/eternal-woods-the-kingdom-of-elenorias-dance-with-nature/
**Public realm:** Kingdom of Elenoria  
**Map-state:** Elenoria  
**Culture(s):** `elenori`  
**Candidate branches:** deep-forest / border-ranger / deep-envoy-elenori  
**Landscape:** ancient broadleaf canopy, root mounds, shifting groves, clean forest streams, shadowed understory  
**Economy and roles:** woodland stewardship, archives, medicine, diplomacy, protected groves, controlled foraging  
**Geometry grammar:** living wood-grown curved structural arches, woven branch walls, stone root foundations, suspended timber walkways, canopy rooms with natural asymmetric growth  
**Canon vs proposal:** Published: grown living wood cities, archives in Aelindril, breathing library walls, patrol paths that shift and sealed collections. Design proposal: detailed habitats and infrastructure.
