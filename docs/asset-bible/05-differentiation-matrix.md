# Cross-regional differentiation matrix

Each row describes silhouette, livelihood and terrain; these three must be visible **before** banners and texture color.

| Area | Dominant structural grammar | Immediately visible economy | Characteristic terrain/transport | Avoid confusion with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Lumeshire](regions/lumeshire.md) | pale dressed stone and pale lime plaster, dark oak beams, tight slate gables, orderly axial courtyards; institutional monuments legible above houses | civic administration, grain, church institutions, road trade, military logistics | temperate farmlands, maintained highways, river crossings, defended forest marches | no identical generic Tudor street everywhere, no magic towers in every hamlet, no forest-swamp buildings used as Imperial civic monuments |
| [Zuraldi](regions/zuraldi.md) | heavy irregular gray stone retaining walls, dark timber frame, deep low slate roofs pitched for wind and rain, siting stepped into steep terrain, rough but engineered terraces | cattle, salted meat, terrace agriculture, mountain ponies, Stonehold-bound caravan exchange, ancestral councils | dense upland forest, gray craggy peaks, terraced valley plots, narrow caravan shelf roads | no Imperial axial grid or gilded cathedral dominance, no generic Alpine resort, no impossible wagons on stair-only roads |
| [Contia](regions/contian.md) | precise pale cut-stone modules, dark basalt detailing, bronze hardware, glazed observatories, geometric plazas, tall measured towers, sober institutional massing | research, archives, licensing, religious law, laboratory crafts, specialized trade | planned stone urban centers, river shelves, maintained roads and scholarly enclaves | no random crooked wizard hut for every citizen, no magical neon skyscrapers, no ubiquitous floating books |
| [Frostgard](regions/frostgard.md) | horizontal heavy-hewn timber, deep steep slate-or-sod pitched roofs, tall smoke vents, rugged stone plinths, windbreak courtyards | winter stores, timber, fishing, furs, defense of giant border, longhall governance | snowy mountain foothills, windswept conifer forests, frozen rivers, sheltered fjord harbors | no universal Viking longship fleets in highland inland burgs, no decorative horns on every roof, no open-air tropical stalls in blizzard |
| [Stonehold](regions/stonehold.md) | carved massive granite/basalt facades, stout squared arches, reinforced vaulted passages, iron/bronze gates, chimney stacks and integrated ore cranes | mining, foundries, metalwork, engineering, stone trade, council and temple oversight | bare high mountain rock, deep mines, cavern avenues, steep pass roads, fortified rock faces | no floating anvils, no implausibly enclosed smelter without air system, no giant statue on every doorway, no uniform golden steampunk |
| [Elenoria](regions/elenoria.md) | living wood-grown curved structural arches, woven branch walls, stone root foundations, suspended timber walkways, canopy rooms with natural asymmetric growth | woodland stewardship, archives, medicine, diplomacy, protected groves, controlled foraging | ancient broadleaf canopy, root mounds, shifting groves, clean forest streams, shadowed understory | no endless rope-bridge theme park without structural roots, no levitating tree houses, no uninhabitable fragile branch nests, no generic bright fairy castle |
| [Grukmar](regions/grukmar.md) | intentionally multiple grammars: marsh goblin settlements use elevated timber piles and reed roofs; upland orc towns use low broad sturdy timber-and-stone halls; mixed markets combine trade modules on raised dry ground | barter, caravan logistics, salvage crafts, fishing/foraging, clan assemblies, intermittent warfare and fortified trading | southern frontier marshes and bogs, salvage streams, grassland uplands, wooded fortified ridges | no single monster shanty style for every Grukmar people, no perpetual skull spikes and arbitrary torture props, no upside-down ramshackle architecture without flood logic, no whole Wyrmreach visual import |
| [Umbra'kor](regions/umbrakor.md) | dark basalt arched stone and cliff-carved tiers, narrow vertical bridges over caverns, iron walkway structure, discreet pale phosphorescent lines and controlled light pockets | house compounds, temple archives, fungus production, intelligence, toll trade and depth defense | vast underground chambers, vertical basalt escarpments, subterranean water, fungus agriculture, heavily guarded rift approaches | no surface Gothic castles inexplicably buried intact, no perpetual neon-purple magical glow, no hanging bridges unsupported, no spider shrine unless explicit local lore approves |
| [Wyrmreach](regions/wyrmreach.md) | NO unified Wyrmreach civilizational style. Coastal lived-in frontier houses vary by local inhabitants; interior remains sparse with squat windbreak refuges, weathered rock markings and rare unnaturally smooth pre-kingdom ruins | rare coastal trade, hardy local subsistence, secretive survey, border watch, isolated ruins; settlement roles vary by people | tempestuous approach sea, hostile gray-crust inland plain, disorienting ridges, unlocated ruins and void-related anomalies | never treat a literal dragon metropolis or cult empire as confirmed canon, never fill the map with ruins every hundred meters, no clear bright sky above the uncharted anomaly zone |

A realm is **not** an asset style. Inhabitants may adopt local architecture, merge settlement craft traditions or share space. Banners, public records and forts communicate the ruler; architecture and livelihood communicate the society and site. Inspect mixed-border examples before assigning any purely cultural default to every building.

## Hybrid scenes to test

- Lumish border town with Zuraldarr caravans: imperial stone bureaucracy, local highland trade modules, shared yards.
- Stonehold border pass with Zuraldarr vendors: mountainous industrial bulk adjacent to smaller slate-roofed trader lodges.
- Grukmar dry uplands and bog market: distinct Orc and Goblin grammars in one realm.
- Contian administrative precinct with Elenori specialists: measured institutional stone around grown-wood courtyard garden.
- Umbra-kori subterranean city with river trade: cavern-house terraces and physically navigable docks; no surface daylight.
- Wyrmreach expedition past inhabited edge: increasingly sparse shelters toward unknown ruins; **uncertainty** remains visible.
